      Camera calibration

      Stand facing the robot at a measured distance until it tracks you, enter the distance and press the button. One distance fixes the offset; a second, a metre or more away, fixes the focal length too.

      Camera angle: how many degrees the camera is tilted, and which way. It decides what counts as floor and what as an obstacle, so a wrong one fills the map with red.

      Speed limits

      Apply to every mode, the autonomous ones included.

      Follow me

      Off, the robot disarms a second after this page stops talking to it. On, follow-me carries on with the phone in your pocket — and so does nothing but the robot's own logic stop it.

      Head

      The head moves only with a pan servo fitted ([live] servo_fitted or --servo) and the MOTORS switch on.
